These pages need extra care. The rules below add to `_kurucu-yeni-alanlar.md`, `_yazar-ortak.md` and

## Religion pages (field "Din ve İnanç")
- Descriptive, comparative and historical only. Never confirm or refute any faith, never rank religions, never say a
  doctrine is true, false, primitive, advanced, rational or irrational. Use "X geleneğine göre …", "Y okulu şunu savunur".
- Give each tradition its own self-description AND the academic-historical view, labelled as such, side by side;
  where they differ, say so plainly without arbitrating ("geleneksel anlatım … ; tarihsel-eleştirel araştırma …").
- Dates of founders, scriptures, councils: give the range scholars actually give and the source; avoid false precision.
- Adherent numbers: one dated, named source (e.g. Pew Research Center 2015/2020 estimates via an allowed domain), with
  uncertainty; no rankings dressed up as judgement.
- Philosophy of religion: every argument is shown with its strongest objections and replies, from SEP/IEP; the page
  maps the debate (premises, which premise each objection attacks); it never announces a winner. Include theistic,
  atheistic and agnostic positions and non-Western traditions (kelâm, Hindu and Buddhist philosophy) where sourced.
- Sacred texts: quote only translations that are public domain (or the original-language text, which is not under
  copyright), ≤ 15 words per quotation; prefer paraphrase.
- Images: no depictions of prophets; prefer maps, timelines, diagrams, calendars.

## Psychiatry and psychometrics (field "Zihin ve Davranış")
- No diagnosis, no screening of the reader, no treatment or medication advice, no "do you have X?" tests. The page is
  about how diagnostic systems are built and how well they work (history, reliability, validity, base rates, the
  statistics of tests), not about the reader's mental health.
- Do not reproduce copyrighted instruments (DSM/ICD criteria text, PHQ-9, BDI, MMPI items, IQ-test items). Describe
  them; use invented, clearly fictional items and data for exercises. Synthetic data must be labelled synthetic.
- Real controversies (reliability of DSM-III field trials vs DSM-5 field trials, categorical vs dimensional models,
  RDoC, HiTOP, Rosenhan 1973 and the later doubts about it, anti-psychiatry) are presented as debates with sources.
- Add a short, calm note with where to seek help (e.g. "bir sağlık profesyoneline başvurun"; in Turkey 112) — no
  hotline numbers you have not verified on an official page.
- Stigmatising language is not used; person-first or neutral terms.

## Consciousness (field "Zihin ve Davranış")
- Theories (global workspace / global neuronal workspace, integrated information theory, higher-order theories,
  recurrent processing, predictive processing, illusionism, panpsychism) are competing schools; give each its claims,
  evidence and criticisms. The 2023 adversarial collaboration (Cogitate) results and the controversy that followed
  (the open letter calling IIT pseudoscience and the replies) are reported as such, without taking sides.
- Experiments on the reader (masking, attentional blink, change blindness, binocular rivalry if feasible) are for
  learning; store nothing, send nothing; say so.

## Literature and cinema (fields "Dil ve Edebiyat", "Sinema")
- Only public-domain works: author/director died before 1956 (Turkey's 70-year rule, safe margin) AND, for US works,
  published 1930 or earlier. State the public-domain basis for every work used. No song lyrics. No copyrighted
  translations; use the original language or a public-domain translation, or your own translation (say so).
- Film: no embedded copyrighted footage or stills. Use diagrams, your own canvas animations, and links to public-domain
  films on allowed domains (loc.gov, archive.org only if allowed — check SOURCE_PREFIXES; report needed domains).
- Analysis methods are presented with their schools (formalism, structuralism, narratology, reader-response…) and
  limitations; no judgement of a work's worth beyond what a sourced critical tradition says, attributed.

## All pages in this group
- Neutral, respectful Turkish. Where a topic is sensitive, prefer understatement and attribution.
